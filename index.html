Features

What You Can Do

Lost Items Community is a lost & found board for the people around you. Posting, searching, chatting and meeting up all happen in one app.

Post What You Lost or Found
Pick “I lost this” or “I found this”, add a title and a category, then pin where it happened on the map. A photo and a few details help the right person recognise it.
Search the Registry
Look through Lost and Found posts by item, place or date, filter by category, or open Nearby to see what's been posted around you.
Chat Before You Meet
Every post has a private chat. Ask about something only the owner would know, and only arrange a meetup once you're both sure.

Why People Use It

It's built for neighbours helping neighbours. It's free, moderators check reported posts, and the safety rules are part of every handover.

Free to use - No fees and no premium plan. You never pay to get your things back.
Notifications that matter - You hear about new messages, claims on your post and moderator decisions.
Moderated community - Anyone can flag a post for a moderator to review, and accounts that break the rules can be suspended.
Community Guidelines

Four Rules That Keep Everyone Safe

The app asks you to accept these before you arrange a handover. Moderators review reported posts and can suspend accounts that break them.

fact_check
Verify before you hand over
Ask the claimant to describe unique features only the owner would know. Confirm in chat first, then arrange the handover.
groups
Meet in a busy public place
Meet during daylight in a place with people around, and bring someone with you if you can. Never invite a stranger to your home.
money_off
Never send money upfront
Lost Items Community is free and no one should ask you to pay for a returned item. Requests for payment or gift cards are a scam signal.
flag
Report anything suspicious
Flag unverified claims, spam or suspicious contact details. A moderator reviews every flagged post and can remove it or suspend the member.
